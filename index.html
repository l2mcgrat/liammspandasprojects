Owen Paget

Small tools for everyday questions.

Owen Paget builds web tools for everyday questions. His first live site, What to Eat, turns dinner indecision into a trip around the world: pick a meal, hover over a country to preview its dishes, or let it surprise you. A creator hub and a stock-monitoring tool are next. Owen is also Liam's longtime rival, from high-school swimming to the First to Four basketball series.

  • What to Eat

    A world map of recipes: choose breakfast, lunch or dinner, hover over a country to preview its dishes, or press Surprise me.

  • Creator Hub Coming soon

    A home for creators and their work.

  • Stock Monitoring Coming soon

    A tool for keeping an eye on the markets.

Respected creator 02 of / 03Mathematics, made visible

3Blue1Brown

Math, explained visually.

Grant Sanderson’s 3Blue1Brown explains math visually. Some lessons build the foundations a STEM undergraduate needs; others are playful explorations of puzzles and beautiful problems. The site adds written and interactive versions of many videos, guided by the idea that loving math begins with deep understanding.

  • About the channel

    Why it exists, who makes it, and answers to common questions, from the animation tools to advice for new math creators.

  • Extras

    Manim, the open-source animation library behind the videos, plus podcast conversations, talks and collaborations.

  • Summer of Math Exposition

    The 2021 open invitation for anyone to publish a math explainer, which drew more than 1,300 entries.

  • Recommendations

    Other places online to practise and explore math: problem collections, interactive articles and fellow channels.

Respected creator 03 of / 03Education, science & curiosity

Complexly

Trustworthy learning, free for everyone.

Founded in 2012 by Hank and John Green, Complexly is a nonprofit making trustworthy educational media that inspires curiosity and lowers barriers to knowledge-building. Its shows include Crash Course, SciShow, PBS Eons and Bizarre Beasts, each shaped by researchers, writers, hosts, artists and fact checkers.

  • Crash Course

    Free, fast-paced courses (more than 50 so far) that accompany high-school and college classes from the humanities to the sciences.

  • SciShow

    Explores the unexpected, covering scientific topics in a fun, accessible way.

  • Crash Course Creator Lab

    A free, four-unit media-literacy project that takes high-school students from research and scripting to a fact-checked video.

  • All shows

    The full directory, from PBS Eons and Journey to the Microcosmos to The Art Assignment and Ours Poetica.

  • Mission & values

    Why Complexly exists: inquiry, narrative and accuracy, and the belief that education is a human right.
