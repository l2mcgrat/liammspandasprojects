03 / Reading the dreams

What keeps showing up.

For fun, not Freud: motifs are counted with a fixed word list over the journal, and the notes below are classic dream-folklore readings of each motif. None of it is clinical, diagnostic or validated. The journal author finds it entertaining; so should you.

Exploration & other worlds

22 matches over 5 nights

Maps, portals and unexplored terrain: the classic reading is a mind busy with ambitions and options not yet taken.

Competition & school

16 matches over 8 nights

Contests, grades and shows: evaluation dreams, the mind re-running old scoreboards (and inventing new ones).

Parties & social scenes

14 matches over 6 nights

Crowded rooms and shifting social standing: dreams rehearsing belonging, status and who is worth the effort.

Pursuit, escape & danger

13 matches over 3 nights

Being chased or breaking out is the textbook avoidance motif: something is being outrun rather than faced.

Work & purpose

11 matches over 2 nights

Even asleep, the analyst justifies the analytics. Work bleeding into dreams usually tracks waking preoccupation.

Water, ice & winter

7 matches over 6 nights

Dream folklore treats water as emotion and ice as emotion put on hold; this journal keeps returning to both.

Games, media & famous faces

6 matches over 2 nights

Media consumed near bedtime shows up nearly verbatim; the journal even hypothesizes this itself on Oct 18.

The evidence desk

How these numbers are made.

  • Source: records/health/sleep/Sleep_and_Dream_Log.txt, read line by line; any unreadable line stops the build.
  • Each paragraph under a date is one dream; "No dreams recorded" is kept as a deliberate entry, not a gap.
  • Emotion chips reuse the site's custom feelings lexicon and VADER polarity; dreams with no cue stay Unclassified rather than being guessed.
  • Motifs count exact word-form matches from a fixed list (shown in the table), so a motif the list misses is simply not counted.
