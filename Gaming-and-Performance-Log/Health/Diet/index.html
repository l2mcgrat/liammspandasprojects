The evidence desk

How these numbers are made.

  • Source: records/health/diet/Diet_and_Calorie_Tracking.txt; any unreadable line stops the build.
  • Deficit = written expenditure − written consumption. Headlines and net lines that disagree with that arithmetic are flagged, never corrected.
  • BMR is the owner's setting of 2,050 kcal for October; expenditure notes in parentheses (walks, cold, dancing) are kept with the day.
  • Macro sums add the per-meal triples; meals without a triple contribute nothing rather than a guess.
