The evidence desk

How these numbers are made.

  • Source: records/athletics/weightlifting/Weightlifting_Log.txt; dropsets are excluded in the log itself.
  • Exercise names are shown with spelling fixed (for example Dumbell → Dumbbell); the raw names stay in the downloads. Differently named lifts are never merged.
  • Assisted lifts record the machine's assistance, so lower numbers mean more bodyweight lifted.
