1 10 October 2026: LeetCode, rap and training data

1.1 Requested changes

  • Bring the code from the leetcode_problems repository into the six LeetCode subsubsections.

  • Bring the music_work repository into the six Rap subsubsections.

  • On the weightlifting charts, a secondary axis for reps, and on the weight axis the heaviest weight managed for at least 10 reps and for at least 12 reps.

  • Pick up the updated swimming and weightlifting logs, and add notes on the site saying where each data file lives, so new sessions can be added without help.

  • Add Asha Langton’s photography site, Small Shots, to the creators showcase (with her permission).

1.2 Implemented changes

  • Each LeetCode destination now lists its solutions, split at the problem headings in the source, with the code as written and a link to the exact lines on GitHub. Lists and Arrays and Trees and Tries take problems by file; Graphs, Recursion, Dynamic Programming and Hard Problems cross-list by a stated rule. Recursion is detected from the code itself. Dynamic Programming has no matching solution yet and says so.

  • Each Rap destination lists its songs with the lyrics as written: Seven Deadly Sins (all four folders), Homewood & Trafalgar (Trafalgar), Made in Abyss, Vagabond, and NanoFtheAbove (Old Classics and My Hero Academia). Future Projects shows the visualizer and audio-conversion scripts. Drafts not yet committed to GitHub are shown without a link.

  • Every weightlifting exercise chart now has a weight axis with three lines (heaviest set, heaviest for 10+ reps, heaviest for 12+ reps) and a reps axis with bars for the reps at the heaviest set; a table and a Progress CSV hold the same values.

  • The 4 October swim (2400 m) is in. The weightlifting log had no entries after 1 October.

  • Each dashboard has an Add a session section: the .txt file to edit, the line format, the latest entry as written, and the rebuild command. The CSV files are exports, not sources. If the older copy of a log beside the local site preview is edited instead, the build now stops and says which file to copy.

  • Small Shots follows Owen’s section in the creators showcase, linking its two collections, Small Things and Things That Make Me Feel Small, plus the Long Exposure and Road Trips sets.

2 4 October 2026: subsection updates and the creators showcase

2.1 Requested changes

  • A copyright notice in the footer and an all-rights-reserved license file.

  • Owen Paget’s creator section, with his photo, What to Eat (live) and two projects to come: Creator Hub and Stock Monitoring.

  • Restructure the Mental Map so its sections, subsections and subsubsections follow the website.

  • A request-a-change form on the Mental Map that drafts an email to l2mcgrat@uwaterloo.ca, with notes for a later database version.

  • This change log and an upcoming-changes document, in separate .tex files under Coding Projects, Website Changes; dates beside the feature cards and a shorter link marker.

  • Running, swimming and weightlifting logs with analytics and LaTeX overviews.

  • Rename the NBA Analytics topic to Sports Analytics, with premade UFC and NBA Predictions vs. Outcomes pages under Predictions.

  • A HIGH JUMP RULES tie-break clarification for Shooting, Backboard and Rim.

  • A PDF guide on the Mental Map for adding Science & Engineering notes from the Gemini chat log.

2.2 Implemented changes

  • Every page footer now carries a copyright line; LICENSE files in both repositories reserve all rights.

  • Owen’s section opens the creators showcase, with his consented photo, the live What to Eat link and two “coming soon” rows.

  • The Mental Map now has six numbered sections, 36 subsections and 216 subsubsections matching the site’s numbering. All earlier prose, 682 display equations and 79 images were moved, not rewritten; 39 destinations already hold notes and 177 are open. Each hexagon’s centre links to its own section.

  • A Request a change button opens a form with name, date, section, subsection, subsubsection and content; submitting opens an email draft. The database plan is in Upcoming Changes.

  • Feature cards show the date each item was added, with a compact Open link.

  • Running, Swimming and Weightlifting pages chart every logged value, link to overview readers and offer JSON and CSV downloads. The I1 map is published without its start marker or coordinates.

  • Sports Analytics replaces NBA Analytics, with the old addresses forwarding; Predictions and Results links to the two new placeholder pages.

  • The rules, their three countback diagrams and the rules PDF now describe the full countback and SUDDEN DEATH.

  • The contribution guide is published on the Mental Map as a reader and a PDF.

3 4 October 2026: the site atlas overhaul (requested 1 October)

3.1 Requested changes

  • A truly hexagonal home page: Science & Engineering at the top, then Business & Economics, Career & Education, Sports & Entertainment, Gaming & Performance Log and Language & Programming, clean on web and mobile, with a hover animation on every symbol.

  • Six topics in each field and six destinations in each topic, laid out the same way with the Mental Map in the centre; physics moved into Science & Engineering.

  • A circular tree of all 259 pages, generated from a nested schema rather than hand-written data.

  • A snap-scrolling showcase of respected creators below the home page, starting with 3Blue1Brown and Complexly; friends only with written consent and no store links.

  • 251 new icons, each with its own animation.

3.2 Implemented changes

  • 265 pages built from one outline, each field and topic a six-position hexagon around the Mental Map; labels that fit at phone widths; hover animations on every node and a one-time play on touch screens.

  • The radial site atlas, drawn recursively from the outline, with a plain-list fallback.

  • The creators showcase with original preview art, scroll snapping and a scroll cue.

  • 259 original animated icons, checked for safety and uniqueness.

  • Existing readers moved to their destinations; 17 old addresses forward with their query strings.
