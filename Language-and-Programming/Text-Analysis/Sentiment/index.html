                                                          Method, provenance & limits

                                                          Source repository · Revision: d00b6fefd7caafb63b36037cff98665c548fa8f1.

                                                          The input is the local working tree. A recorded HEAD commit is provenance, not proof of a clean checkout; linked source may differ from the analyzed text.

                                                          66 lyric files → 66 retained song versions; 0 normalized duplicate files are aliases, not extra observations. All versions with differing text are kept. Repeated verses remain counted.

                                                          Read the exact analysis rules

                                                          Local UTF-8 text working tree; .git, ._ metadata, symlinks/junctions and non-.txt files excluded. No source is executed or modified.

                                                          NFC/casefold letter forms with internal normalized apostrophes; digits, punctuation, underscores and hyphens delimit. Timestamps and standalone section headers excluded. No stemming; stopwords flagged, never removed.

                                                          One record per nonblank physical line except standalone headers/timestamps; original text and 1-based line number preserved. No sentence segmentation, even for long paragraphs.

                                                          Exact equality after NFC/apostrophe normalization, timestamp/header/blank-line removal and per-line whitespace collapse. Case, punctuation and lyric-line boundaries retained. First sorted path is canonical; aliases excluded from all corpus metrics.

                                                          Nonidentical versions, repeated verses and shared phrases remain, so revisions/repetition and collection sizes bias frequencies. Counts are not independent songs or representative language samples.

                                                          Custom English lexicon text categories, not diagnosis or an emotion model. Every longest nonoverlapping cue occurrence scores 1 per mapped branch; negated cues score 0 and stay in evidence. Scores are not probabilities; unclassified means no active cues, not neutral feelings.

                                                          Suppress on a negator in the preceding 3 tokens, stopping at . ! ? ; : , em dash or contrast words. Internal n't contractions included; 'not only/just' exempt. No inversion, double-negation logic or post-cue negation; multiword cues do not cross clause punctuation.

                                                          Sum branch scores by family; choose largest family total then largest branch. Ties use checked-in family/branch order only. All matches and family scores retained; tiedEmotions lists top family ties. ambiguous means multiple active families or tied primary branches.

                                                          Exploratory heuristic, not calibrated confidence: no syntax, sarcasm, figurative/slang, speaker or quotation resolution. Family/branch names including Depressed describe text cues only; omissions and false matches are expected.

                                                          vaderSentiment on timestamp-cleaned original physical lines (case/punctuation retained); compound/neg/neu/pos are VADER polarity outputs only, not feelings-wheel classification.
