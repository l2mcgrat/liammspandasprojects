02 / How it works

Strength from 1 to 10, moved by notes.

  • Add a person to a group with a starting strength. Groups start as Collingwood friends and family, University friends, Work and LinkedIn; type a new group name to add one.
  • After a call, a visit or a long silence, add a note and choose Stronger, Same or Weaker. The strength moves one step and the note is dated.
  • Connect two people who know each other to draw the line between them.
  • Each change is encrypted and signed in the browser before it leaves the device.
  • It is kept on the device, sent through a relay that holds it for 12 hours, and stored in the site's repository by a job that runs every hour.
  • Another device picks a change up the next time it syncs: from the relay inside those 12 hours, and from the repository once the hourly job has stored it.
  • The repository and the relay only ever hold ciphertext. The job that stores it can check the signature and cannot read the content.
