02 / CLI & automated GitHub Actions sync

How zero-token deterministic repair + cloud compilation work end-to-end.

When you log workouts or notes on your phone in Google Docs, the deterministic parser repairs common mobile typing slips at 0 token cost — including missing colons/semicolons after exercise names (Incline Dumbbell Press 10x40lbs), dash or comma separators (Barbell Press - 25x45lbs, Seated Cable Rows, 15x58lbs), singular lb units, month headers (September 2026), and year typos (10/25/2025 after 10/24/2026).

python -B -m site_content.log_sync && python -B -m site_content.build --also-local

Owner Passkey & Admin Vault · SHA-256 HMAC + Session/IP Lock

Unlock Admin

Unlocking Admin is valid for 30 minutes across all branches of the site (as long as you stay within the site's URL tree) and is bound to your current IP address. Your Master Passphrase always works forever (4,096-round SHA-256 HMAC; never stored in plaintext). Once unlocked, use the 🔑 Passwords button in the top bar to create or revoke temporary friend passwords.

Admin Access & Delegated Passwords · Master Passkey Protected

Create or Revoke Access Passwords

Your Master Passphrase is immutable and always works forever—creating a friend/guest password never overwrites your Master Passphrase. Delegated passwords default to 12 hours before automatic revocation, or can be extended up to Infinite (∞) and revoked at any time.

Active Delegated Passwords

No delegated passwords active — only your Master Passphrase is enabled.
