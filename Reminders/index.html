Why This Tab Exists · The REABS Manifesto (Verbatim)

REABS — Random Extra Additional Bullshit

“I want to throw something new in for the admin tabs, something similar to workbench called Reminders, which is different from to do which is more website focused. These will be things I have to get done, all it Random Extra Additional Bullshit, or REABS, which is stuff I have to do ontop of stuff that actually benefits my future directly as a human, stuff which is outside of Financial Management, Natural Formal and Social Sciences/Engineering/Business/Macro/Micro and SocioEconomic Study/Theory applications, website To Dos, Rap Lyrics, Voice Memo Sessions, you know, stuff that benefits my understanding of the universe, the world, the economy on a macroscopic and microscopic or business level, doesn't help me understand myself and optimize my interactions with people and the world at large... no this is just Random Extra Additional Bullshit that poses no benefit to me whatsoever but needs to get done. Put this rant in the page too this part included!”

“So because these things are random and have little to no connection to my actual development as a person, or goals, week to week or month to month personal data study or website optimization, I need reminders at specific times of the day to do certain things.”
