01 / The Iceberg split

Why visible tech jobs are only 2.2% of the story — and 11.7% sits underwater.

Headlines track AI through software engineers and data centers — the 2.2% visible tip. Project Iceberg maps 32,000+ occupational skills across 923 occupations and finds 11.7% of total wage value (~$1.2 trillion) in submerged cognitive, administrative, financial, and analytical tasks inside non-tech industries.

■ Visible tip (direct tech/computing adoption)■ Submerged Iceberg (skills-centered cognitive/admin exposure)

02 / Inside the industries

How the six occupational role families divide every sector.

An industry is not a single job. Manufacturing is 54% skilled trades and plant operators (low AI overlap), but 25% analytical engineering and clerical procurement/scheduling (high submerged overlap). Healthcare is 56% hands-on clinical care, yet 18% administrative billing, coding, and intake.
